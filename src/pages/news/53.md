## Global Update: The Leaderboards are Here!

The long-awaited competitive edge has arrived. You can now see where you stand among thousands of users across the entire Wolium network or check who’s the most active member on your specific server.

### New Features
- **Global & Server Rankings:** Toggle between "World" view to see the top users globally or "Server" view to focus on your community.
- **Multiple Metrics:** Rankings are no longer just about balance. Compete in:
    - **Economy:** Total Balance, Bank, and Cash.
    - **Progression:** Level, Total XP, and Upgrades.
    - **Engagement:** Message counts, Voice time, and Activity time.
    - **Social:** Vote counts and Streaks.
- **Top Servers List:** A dedicated scope to see which Discord servers are the most active and wealthy in the ecosystem.
- **Personal Rank Highlighting:** Your own position is pinned at the bottom of the list, so you always know how many steps are left to the top.

### Improved
- **Advanced Number Formatting:** Numbers now use readable suffixes (K, M, B, T...) and even scientific notation for extreme values. No more guessing how many zeros are on your screen!
- **Smarter Duration Display:** Time spent in voice or activities is now formatted more precisely, from seconds to years.
- **Discord Asset Caching:** We’ve optimized how server icons and user avatars are loaded. If a Discord asset is temporarily unavailable, the system will now provide a smooth offline fallback instead of a broken image.
- **Mobile Navigation:** The leaderboard features a responsive pagination system and quick-action chips for switching metrics on the go.

### Fixed
- **Profile Metric Consistency:** Fixed several UI bugs where XP and level numbers in the profile didn't match the internal database values due to rounding errors.
