## Settings redesign: privacy controls and data management

The settings page has been rebuilt from scratch. It now lets you manage your privacy preferences, control what data is stored, and delete it — all in one place.

### Added
- **Privacy settings:** A new section lets you toggle exactly what Wolium tracks — messages, voice, activity, diagnostics, and profile visibility
- **Privacy presets:** Three one-click presets to quickly apply a configuration — *Private* (nothing saved), *Balanced* (basic stats only), or *Full Analytics* (everything enabled)
- **Data management:** Individual delete buttons for each data category — messages, voice, activities, economy, and analytics — without affecting the rest
- **Danger zone:** A separate section to permanently delete all your data at once, with a double confirmation step
- **Number format:** You can now choose how large numbers are displayed — normal (1.23K), plain (1,234), or scientific (1.23e3)
- **Activity run count:** When chart bars represent multiple activity sessions merged together, the tooltip now shows how many runs are included

### Improved
- Profile stats now load faster — the page no longer waits up to 80 seconds before showing an error
- Skeleton loading animation now correctly covers the elements it belongs to, without bleeding into surrounding layout
