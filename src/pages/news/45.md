## Navigation and modal improvements

A small interface update was released for navigation and modal tabs.

### Changed
- **News** now appears before **Settings** in the sidebar
- **News** now uses its own sidebar icon

### Improved
- Tabs in **profile chart** now handle long labels better
- Long tab names in **profile chart** are truncated instead of overflowing
- The tabs area in **profile chart** becomes scrollable when there is not enough space
