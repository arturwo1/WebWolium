## News page, filters, and localization update

The website now includes a dedicated **News** section.

- News page in the website navigation
- Individual news post pages
- Filters by:
  - source
  - priority
  - type
  - time
- Pagination with page jump
- URL state for filters and pages

- Localized news labels and page titles
- News card and post layout
- Hover effects for cards on the homepage and news page

- Existing news posts were moved to simpler cross-platform file names
