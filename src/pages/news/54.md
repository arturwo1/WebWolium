## Wolium v3.1.0 — Smarter AI, Leaderboards & More

This update focuses on making the AI feel more natural and aware of your server, bringing competitive leaderboards to the website, and squashing a number of stability issues.

---

### 🤖 Smarter AI

The AI assistant got a significant upgrade across the board.

- **Server emoji awareness:** The bot now knows which custom emojis exist on your server and can use them naturally in its responses — making conversations feel much more at home.
- **Long-term memory:** The bot can now remember important things about you and your server between sessions. No more repeating yourself every time.
- **Moderation confirmation:** Any moderation action triggered through AI (bans, kicks, mutes, warns) now requires you to explicitly confirm it first. Accidental punishments are a thing of the past.
- **Faster responses:** Tool usage — looking up messages, profiles, reactions — has been optimized to feel noticeably snappier.

---

### 📊 Leaderboards on the Website

- Compete globally across the entire Wolium network, or focus on your own server.
- Rankings cover a wide range of metrics: balance, XP, level, voice time, messages, activity time, votes, streaks, and more.
- Large numbers and durations are displayed in a clean, readable format — 1.4M instead of 1,400,000, or "2h 34m" instead of a raw second count.

---

### 🌍 Translations

- Translations now load from a startup cache, so the bot is ready faster after a restart.
- New or missing translations are now handled in the background in batches — nothing blocks the bot while it works.
- Fixed an edge case where an empty cached translation could be mistakenly used instead of fetching the real one.

---

### ⚙️ Stability

- Fixed several crashes that could occur when joining servers or loading user data.
- Duplicate slash commands are now automatically cleaned up on startup — no more seeing the same command twice.
- Activity sessions shorter than 60 seconds are no longer saved, keeping your stats accurate and free of noise.
- Fixed a bug where the AI could fail to send a reply if the original message was deleted.
