## Wolium uptime schedule

Wolium has been running from my PC since **September 27, 2023**.

### During study periods
- **Mon–Fri:** ~08:00 to ~23:00–03:00
- **Sat–Sun:** ~10:00–13:00 to ~23:00–05:00

### During holidays
- **Mon–Sun:** ~09:00–15:00 to ~00:00–06:30

If the bot is offline, the most likely reasons are:
- I forgot to start it
- I do not have enough time
- There is a technical issue
